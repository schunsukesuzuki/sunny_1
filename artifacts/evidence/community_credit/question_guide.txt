Interview Question Guide1. How was money talked about in your house growing up? a. What are your earliest memories of using money?2. Can you tell me a little about your financial situation today?a. What keeps you awake at night or worries you when you think about your finances?b. Is there something you are particularly proud of financially?3. Say you have a financial crisis (like an unexpected medical expense) that you cannot afford on your own—where do you turn? (loan, emergency fund, help from family, social services)?a. Do you feel that you have support in your financial life, or do you feel like you are going it alone?4. Do you use a bank or a credit union? Which one? How did you choose? If not, why not?a. Do you like your bank or credit union or other financial provider? Do you trust them? Why/why not?5. When you have the option, do you prefer to handle banking and financial services online or inperson? Why? If you do go online, how do you assess if something is legitimate?6. Are there other places you go besides a bank/credit union to help you handle your bills andother financial matters? (payday lender, check cashers, pawn shops, crypto)7. Do you have any kind of loans or credit card debt right now? What are they? How did you getthem (i.e., who provides them)? (Consider: mortgages, credit card, car loan, student loan, BNPL, payday...)a. What ads can you remember seeing recently about financial products like loans? Were they on billboards, on your phone, at an ATM, at a store, somewhere else?8. What do you wish your primary financial institution would do for you (i.e., what kind of services or products do you wish they offered)?a. Have you ever stopped using a product or provider because of a bad experience?9. Who or what do you trust to give you advice on financial topics like taking out a loan (people you know, public figures, professionals, etc.)? Who or what do you not trust? Why?10. Have you ever been the victim of a financial scam?
